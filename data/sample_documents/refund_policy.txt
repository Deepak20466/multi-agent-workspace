Refund Policy

Our refund policy allows returns within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Refunds are processed
within 5 business days of receiving the returned item, and are issued to the
original payment method.

Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable. Shipping costs are
non-refundable unless the return is due to a defect or shipping error on our
part. Contact support@example.com to initiate a return.
